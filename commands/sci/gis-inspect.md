---
description: "Inspect geospatial data: CRS, extent, bands, layers."
argument-hint: "<file>"
allowed-tools: Bash
---

Inspect the geospatial file in $ARGUMENTS using the `gis-geospatial` skill. Detect gdal/ogr, report CRS/projection, extent, resolution/bands (raster) or geometry/fields (vector), and flag CRS mismatches or missing overviews. Read-only.
