# Tech Writing

**Category:** quality

## Checklist

- Structured for the reader's task
- Concrete, runnable examples
- Jargon defined or avoided

## Common pitfalls

- Wall-of-text with no structure
- Explaining what without why
- Assuming reader context they lack
