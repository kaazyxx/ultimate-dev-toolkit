# Estimation Planning

**Category:** quality

## Checklist

- Work decomposed into small pieces
- Estimates as ranges with uncertainty
- Scope negotiable against time

## Common pitfalls

- Single-point estimates presented as certain
- Ignoring unknowns/risk
- No buffer for the unexpected
