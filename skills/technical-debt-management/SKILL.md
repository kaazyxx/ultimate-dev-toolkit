# Technical Debt Management

**Category:** quality

## Checklist

- Debt made visible (tracked, not tribal)
- Prioritized by risk/interest
- Paid down incrementally with features

## Common pitfalls

- Big-bang rewrites instead of incremental
- Debt invisible until it explodes
- Refactoring with no test safety net
