# Statistics Fundamentals

**Category:** data-ml

## Checklist

- Assumptions of the test checked
- Effect size, not just p-value
- Multiple-comparison correction where needed

## Common pitfalls

- p-hacking / cherry-picking
- Confusing correlation with causation
- Ignoring sample size/power
