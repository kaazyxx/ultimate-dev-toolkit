# Architecture Decision Records

**Category:** quality

## Checklist

- Context and alternatives recorded
- Decision and consequences explicit
- Stored with the code, versioned

## Common pitfalls

- Decisions living only in people's heads
- ADRs written but never updated
- No record of rejected alternatives
