## Checklist

- Chart type fits the data/question
- Axes honest (no truncation to mislead)
- Colorblind-safe palettes

## Common pitfalls

- 3D/pie charts obscuring data
- Truncated y-axis exaggerating differences
- Overplotting without transparency/binning
